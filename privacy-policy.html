MotionEase Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how the MotionEase Android app ("MotionEase", "the app") handles information. MotionEase is published by The Work Builder ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact us at trackit1225@gmail.com.

Summary

  • MotionEase does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Motion sensor readings are processed only on your device, in real time, to move the on-screen cues. They are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

Information processed on your device

Motion sensors. MotionEase reads your device's accelerometer, gravity and linear-acceleration sensors while motion cues or the Horizon tool are in use. These readings are used immediately to draw the cues and are then discarded. They never leave your device.

Settings. Your preferences (such as dot size, colour and sensitivity) are stored locally on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Permissions the app uses

  • Display over other apps: draws the motion cue dots on top of the app you are using. It cannot see or read what other apps show.
  • Run in the foreground and show notifications: keeps the cues running while you use other apps and shows a notification with a "Turn off" button.
  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads and the consent form.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for advertising, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

MotionEase shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and UK). To serve, personalise (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city, not precise location)
  • Information about how you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data related to ads

This data is encrypted in transit. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws). Where required by law, MotionEase asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under About > Privacy and ad choices.
  • Personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads).

Children

MotionEase is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive or store the data that Google's advertising services collect. Apart from the advertising described above, MotionEase does not share information with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any personal data about you on our servers. Local settings are removed when you uninstall the app. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com or reset your advertising ID as described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to or restrict its processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests are best directed to Google for advertising data. You can still contact us at trackit1225@gmail.com and we will help.

Security

MotionEase keeps processing on your device wherever possible. Network connections for ads use encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when features change. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
