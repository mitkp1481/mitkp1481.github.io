MotionEase

Motion sickness relief for your phone

MotionEase helps with motion sickness while you use your phone on the move. Dots at the edges of your screen move with the vehicle, over any app you're using, so what you see matches what you feel.

  • Motion cues over any app, from a widget, Quick Settings tile or the app
  • Horizon: a live horizon that stays level with the real world
  • Breathe: a calm, paced breathing guide
